# Danbooru tag group: image composition

Source: https://danbooru.donmai.us/wiki_pages/tag_group%3Aimage_composition (fetched 2026-09-30). This is the camera, framing and technique vocabulary; for a manga panel these are the tags that come after the character and scene tags. Every entry is a tag: lowercase it and join words with underscores (`from_above`, `cowboy_shot`, `motion_lines`). Section headings are the wiki's own.

## For a manga panel, the short list

* **View angle**: from_above, from_below, from_behind, from_side, straight-on, dutch_angle, high_up
* **Framing the body**: close-up, portrait, upper_body, cowboy_shot, full_body, wide_shot, very_wide_shot, profile, feet_out_of_frame, head_out_of_frame
* **Depth and lens**: depth_of_field, bokeh, foreshortening, fisheye, perspective, vanishing_point, panorama
* **Motion and emphasis**: motion_lines, speed_lines, emphasis_lines, motion_blur, afterimage
* **Light**: backlighting, sidelighting, underlighting, lens_flare, silhouette, chiaroscuro, bloom, high_contrast
* **Comic form**: comic, 4koma, 2koma, borderless_panels, right-to-left_comic, silent_comic, letterboxed, border
* **Focus**: solo_focus, face_focus, hand_focus, eye_focus, object_focus, male_focus
* **Style words that steer NoobAI**: lineart, sketch, painterly, realistic, screentones, halftone, monochrome, greyscale (the last two belong in the negative prompt for a colour page)
* **Flaws to keep in the negative prompt**: bad_anatomy, bad_hands, bad_feet, bad_proportions, extra_digits, blurry, jpeg_artifacts, watermark, artistic_error, lowres
